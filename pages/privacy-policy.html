Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

At Elysium Cocktail Lounge, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide to us.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide when you use our website, including when you contact us, request a private room booking, or sign up for a cocktail class.

Depending on the form you use, this information may include your name, email address, phone number, requested booking information, number of guests, and any message or additional information you choose to provide.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to questions and requests.
  • Process private room and event requests.
  • Manage cocktail class registrations.
  • Send booking or request confirmations.
  • Communicate with you regarding a request you submitted.
  • Maintain and improve our website and services.

Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may use trusted service providers to operate portions of our website and communications. These providers may process information on our behalf only as necessary to provide their services.

Our website infrastructure may use services such as Supabase for database and application functionality and Resend for transactional email delivery.

Contact and Booking Information

Information submitted through our contact, private-room, and cocktail-class forms is used to process and respond to your request.

We retain this information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain appropriate business records, or as otherwise required by law.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information submitted through our website. However, no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies when necessary for website functionality, security, or performance.

We do not use information collected through cookies to sell or rent your personal information.

Third-Party Services

Our website may rely on third-party services to provide hosting, database functionality, email delivery, security, or other website features. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Your Choices

You may contact us if you have questions about personal information you have submitted through our website or if you would like to request that we review or delete information that we maintain, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Elysium Cocktail Lounge through our Contact page .
